\section*{Problem 1 - Washing Machine Mount Design}


The Matlab code for this problem is listed in Appendix~\ref{appendix:problem1}.


\subsection*{Problem 1a}

Equation~\ref{equation:1aForcingFunction} calculates the force produced by the imbalanced 1kg mass at a 0.5 $m$ radius from the center of the washing machine as a function of angular velocity,

\vspace{-0.25cm}
\begin{equation}
    F = m \cdot \omega^2 \cdot r
\end{equation}
\label{equation:1aForcingFunction}

where $m$ is 0.5 $kg$, $\omega$ is the angular velocity in $\frac{radians}{s}$, and $r$ is 0.5 $m$.

\vspace{0.25cm}
Figure~\ref{figure:1aLoadForceVersusRPM} shows the force produced by the 1 kg load as a function of angular velocity in RPM.

\vspace{-0.25cm}
\begin{figure}[htb!]
    \center
        \includegraphics[scale = 0.7, keepaspectratio]{Homework_3_Part_1a.pdf}
    \caption{Load force versus washing machine angular speed.}
    \label{figure:1aLoadForceVersusRPM}
\end{figure}

\vspace{0.25cm}
At 300 RPM, or 5 Hz, the imbalanced load force amplitude is 493.5 N.



\subsection*{Problem 1b}

The required spring stiffness, $k_s$, is 9,810 $\frac{N}{m}$.  The mount's natural frequency, $w_o$, is 1.5 $Hz$.
