\subsection*{Spectrogram of Recording at the 23$^\circ$ Recording Angle}

\vspace{-0.2cm}
Figure~\ref{figure:2spectrogram} shows the spectrogram for the Channel 1 pressure recording at an angle of 23$^\circ$.

\vspace{-0.2cm}
\begin{figure}[htb!]
    \vspace{-0.1cm}
    \center
        \includegraphics[scale = 0.7, keepaspectratio]{Spectrogram.pdf}
    \caption{Spectrogram for the pressure recording at 23$^\circ$.}
    \label{figure:2spectrogram}

The bottom trace in the spectrogram, which corresponds to the shaft rotation, is about 58.6 cycles per second.



\vspace{-0.2cm}
\subsection*{Estimate of Shaft Rotation Speed}

\vspace{-0.2cm}
Figure~\ref{figure:3rpmEstimate} shows the estimated rotation speed of the shaft.

\vspace{-0.2cm}
\begin{figure}[htb!]
    \vspace{-0.1cm}
    \center
        \includegraphics[scale = 0.7, keepaspectratio]{RPM Estimate.pdf}
    \caption{Rotation speed estimation.}
    \label{figure:3rpmEstimate}
\end{figure}

The maximum shaft rotation speed is approximately 58.9 cycles per second, or 3,532 RPM.  This maximum rotation speed occurs in revolution 1,672.
